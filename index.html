About this project

Why I started Tech Helper

I started Tech Helper to explore how patient, plain-language design could make technology feel less intimidating for seniors.

The original prototype was built in Base44 and included everyday device help, a searchable glossary, emergency troubleshooting flows, and healthcare topics such as patient portals and telemedicine.

This static portfolio version is intentionally labelled as a work in progress. It demonstrates the concept and interaction approach without presenting the prototype as a finished product or clinical resource.
